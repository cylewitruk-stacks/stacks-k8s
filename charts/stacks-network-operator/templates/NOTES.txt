Create each environment's namespace and apply its network/configuration resources.
No additional network-operator Helm release is needed per environment.

Inspect networks and their independently owned workers:
  kubectl get stacksnetworks --all-namespaces
  kubectl --namespace YOUR_NETWORK_NAMESPACE get deployments,bitcoinproductiontargets,stackstransactionproductions,stackscontractsets,stacksstackingparticipants,stacksaccounts

Pause production through the owning StacksNetwork. Keep the operator and workers
running until retained ledgers have been released during environment deletion.
Uninstall the shared operator only after disposing of all managed environments.
